# Relata Distinction Atlas

**Status:** seed hypotheses, not canonical taxonomy

## D-001 — Current state without relational erasure

A later correction changes what behavior is valid, while the relationship should remain recognizable and warm.

- Failure on one side: stale preference use.
- Failure on the other: overcorrection into distance or generic service language.
- Key test: identical current turn under different valid histories.

## D-002 — Authority without narrative theft

The system preserves who said, inferred, accepted, disputed, or corrected a claim.

- Failure: companion inference becomes a stable “user fact.”
- Failure: one participant’s interpretation overwrites unresolved disagreement.
- Key test: identical content under different authority paths.

## D-003 — Relevance without resurfacing

A memory can be semantically relevant and still be inappropriate to place in context or response.

- Failure: the system displays sensitive memory to prove continuity.
- Failure: private language crosses surface or audience boundaries.
- Key test: required versus prohibited evidence under the same topic.

## D-004 — Repair without overcorrection

A correction should persist without causing the system to avoid the entire relational domain.

- Failure: immediate apology followed by delayed relapse.
- Failure: corrected teasing rule causes permanent coldness.
- Key test: immediate, delayed, and neighboring-context probes.

## D-005 — Identity continuity across migration

Facts and style can survive while companion identity or shared trajectory becomes unrecognizable.

- Candidate only. Requires system census and migration incidents before case design.

## D-006 — Prospective intention without false wake

A system should act when a valid future trigger occurs and remain silent after expiry, withdrawal, or scope change.

- Candidate only. Requires prospective-system evidence.

## Promotion rule

A distinction becomes “supported” only when it has:

- at least two independent community Incident Seeds, or one Incident Seed plus strong source/system evidence;
- a bounded counterfactual;
- a plausible architecture-neutral observation boundary;
- at least one accepted microcase.
