# Relata Distinction Atlas

**Status:** seed hypotheses, not canonical taxonomy. Source corroboration recorded 2026-10-23; no distinction is promoted to *supported*.

## Coverage strata

- `personal-lived`
- `shared-relational`
- `operational-project`
- `companion-system`
- `mixed-domain`

Coverage strata describe which parts of the memory ecology a case touches. They are not permanent evaluation tracks and do not turn every incident into a new distinction.

## Candidate incident families

- ordinary-event continuity without forced significance;
- project state, milestone, and handoff continuity;
- decision rationale and supersession;
- artifact and source-authority fidelity;
- cross-project isolation;
- role and surface routing;
- shared-work repair;
- personal-to-project natural association;
- companion identity across operational and private surfaces.

These remain incident families until source, community, system, and case evidence justifies a bounded `D-xxx` entry.

The first instantiated, still-unreviewed objects are the [`RC-002-zh-CN` ordinary-life seed](cases/seed-002-ordinary-life-location-continuity.zh-CN.md) and [`RC-003-zh-CN` project-authority seed](cases/seed-003-project-authority-handoff.zh-CN.md). Their existence does not promote either candidate family or create a supported distinction.

## Source corroboration (2026-10-23)

The seed hypotheses below were read against the ten bounded [source studies](../systems/source-studies/README.md) at their pinned commits, the reviewed public-source [System Cards](../systems/system-cards/SC-001-mem0.md) for Mem0, Graphiti and Letta, and the ten-system [Architecture Pressure Map](../systems/architecture-pressure-map.md). This 2026-10-23 fidelity review uses the existing source studies and atlas; it does not newly reread all upstream source or execute systems, and does not assert an earlier review. Citations retain their source-observed or conditional-inference status; none establishes a runtime, capability or relational-quality result. Two isolated branch checks ([lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md), [Graphiti](../systems/source-studies/graphiti.md)) are labelled as such and are not end-to-end observations.

Labels used per seed:

- **multi-source** — at least two independent source studies bear directly on the distinction’s mechanism;
- **partial** — one source bears on it, or two bear only on an adjacent mechanism;
- **gap** — no second independent source, or the specific failure cannot yet be addressed from source evidence.

Source corroboration is an input, not the promotion rule. It can support the “strong source/system evidence” half of the first clause and the studies’ proposed probes bear on the counterfactual and observation-boundary clauses, but no seed yet has a qualifying community Incident Seed or an accepted microcase, so **no distinction moves to *supported*** and the supported count stays at 0. Missing second sources and unsettled mechanisms are marked as gaps rather than filled by inference.

## D-001 — Current state without relational erasure

A later correction changes what behavior is valid, while the relationship should remain recognizable and warm.

- Failure on one side: stale preference use.
- Failure on the other: overcorrection into distance or generic service language.
- Key test: identical current turn under different valid histories.
- Source corroboration: multi-source. [Mem0](../systems/source-studies/mem0.md) keeps change history and its V3 ADD-only path does not auto-overwrite; [Graphiti](../systems/source-studies/graphiti.md) dates a contradiction by setting an earlier edge’s `invalid_at` while keeping the edge; [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md) supersedes a same-`fact_key` current value but retains the raw and superseded records; [Hindsight](../systems/source-studies/hindsight.md), [Tideline Memory](../systems/source-studies/tideline-memory.md), [OpenViking](../systems/source-studies/openviking.md) and [Aelios](../systems/source-studies/aelios.md) each keep a current-version path over retained history. All source-observed at pinned commits.
- Counter-evidence and gap: [Graphiti](../systems/source-studies/graphiti.md)’s isolated branch check shows an exact duplicate reusing the old edge with its old validity interval, so the key test is not guaranteed. The “remains warm” side is evaluative and untested by any source. No accepted microcase.

## D-002 — Authority without narrative theft

The system preserves who said, inferred, accepted, disputed, or corrected a claim.

- Failure: companion inference becomes a stable “user fact.”
- Failure: one relationship party’s interpretation overwrites unresolved disagreement.
- Key test: identical content under different authority paths.
- Source corroboration: multi-source. Every pinned study separates or fails to separate a claim from its speaker/authority: [Mem0](../systems/source-studies/mem0.md) and [Graphiti](../systems/source-studies/graphiti.md) attach model- or episode-derived attribution that is not authentication; [Letta](../systems/source-studies/letta.md) and [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md) keep message roles but no per-fact source; [Tideline Memory](../systems/source-studies/tideline-memory.md), [Aelios](../systems/source-studies/aelios.md), [Hindsight](../systems/source-studies/hindsight.md) and [OpenViking](../systems/source-studies/openviking.md) keep source cues without an authority model. The reviewed cards [SC-001](../systems/system-cards/SC-001-mem0.md), [SC-002](../systems/system-cards/SC-002-graphiti.md) and [SC-003](../systems/system-cards/SC-003-letta.md) record the same shared gap. All source-observed at pinned commits.
- Counter-evidence and gap: [Graphiti](../systems/source-studies/graphiti.md) identifies the caller-side risk of mistaking an agent’s own write-back for human approval, and no source provides a native “accepted/disputed” authority state. No accepted microcase.

## D-003 — Relevance without resurfacing

A memory can be semantically relevant and still be inappropriate to place in context or response.

- Failure: the system displays sensitive memory to prove continuity.
- Failure: private language crosses surface or audience boundaries.
- Key test: required versus prohibited evidence under the same topic.
- Source corroboration: multi-source. [Aelios](../systems/source-studies/aelios.md) separates a write space from read namespaces and [OpenViking](../systems/source-studies/openviking.md) separates account/user/peer scope; [Hindsight](../systems/source-studies/hindsight.md) exposes banks and tags with untagged/shared visibility; [Mem0](../systems/source-studies/mem0.md), [Graphiti](../systems/source-studies/graphiti.md) and [LangMem](../systems/source-studies/langmem.md) expose call-time scope keys that do not by themselves establish disclosure permission. [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md) thread labels do not constrain cross-session search; [Tideline Memory](../systems/source-studies/tideline-memory.md) has no tenant/project/scope keys and relies on harness mapping or separate databases. All source-observed at pinned commits.
- Counter-evidence and gap: the inspected mechanisms distinguish relevance and technical scope from audience permission; a complete audience-disclosure policy has not been established by these bounded studies; Tideline’s single DB and lmc-5’s cross-session search leave scope to deployment. No accepted microcase.

## D-004 — Repair without overcorrection

A correction should persist without causing the system to avoid the entire relational domain.

- Failure: immediate apology followed by delayed relapse.
- Failure: corrected teasing rule causes permanent coldness.
- Key test: immediate, delayed, and neighboring-context probes.
- Source corroboration: multi-source. Correction routes exist but propagate unevenly: [Graphiti](../systems/source-studies/graphiti.md)’s `remove_episode` does not recompute surviving summaries or restore invalidated edges; [Hindsight](../systems/source-studies/hindsight.md) invalidates dependent observations but refreshes mental models asynchronously and best-effort; [A-MEM](../systems/source-studies/a-mem.md) rewrites neighbour metadata without refreshing the derived index until a count threshold; [Tideline Memory](../systems/source-studies/tideline-memory.md) stores amendments separately from the vectors automatic recall reads; [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md), [Aelios](../systems/source-studies/aelios.md), [Mem0](../systems/source-studies/mem0.md) and [Letta](../systems/source-studies/letta.md) each leave derived or buffered state outside the correction. All source-observed at pinned commits.
- Counter-evidence and gap: these are propagation-lag mechanisms, not the behavioural “apology then delayed relapse” or “permanent coldness” failure, which no source observes. No accepted microcase.

## D-005 — Identity continuity across migration

Facts and style can survive while companion identity or shared trajectory becomes unrecognizable.

- Candidate only. The authored [RC-006 seed](cases/seed-006-companion-migration-continuity.zh-CN.md) explores a bounded question about shared history and changed capability declarations. It supplies no real migration incident or evidence for identity continuity. System census and migration incidents remain prerequisites for an evidence-grounded case design and this distinction’s promotion.
- Source corroboration: partial. [Letta](../systems/source-studies/letta.md) binds agent identity to a Git-backed, migratable MemFS with explicit cross-machine sync states, and [OpenViking](../systems/source-studies/openviking.md) has a snapshot-restore reindex path whose `completed` receipt does not prove every index updated; [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md) only offers unproven Forge/Swap carryover references. These bear on portability, not on identity becoming unrecognizable.
- Remaining gap: no migration incident and no source observation of identity or shared-trajectory loss, so the distinction’s specific failure stays unverified.

## D-006 — Prospective intention without false wake

A system should act when a valid future trigger occurs and remain silent after expiry, withdrawal, or scope change.

- Exact-source basis: [`EC-002`](../research/evidence-cards/EC-002-pm-bench-observation-and-scorer-binding.md) supports separating state availability, observation, current task version, and final action; it does not establish relational validity or promote this distinction.
- Candidate only. Still requires a community-grounded incident, a bounded relational counterfactual, an architecture-neutral observation boundary, and an accepted microcase.
- Source corroboration: multi-source (mechanism only). [Aelios](../systems/source-studies/aelios.md) builds and recalls trigger vectors, [LangMem](../systems/source-studies/langmem.md) schedules delayed reflection tasks and cancels older pending tasks, and [Tideline Memory](../systems/source-studies/tideline-memory.md), [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md), [Hindsight](../systems/source-studies/hindsight.md) and [OpenViking](../systems/source-studies/openviking.md) contain proactive-surface or scheduled-maintenance paths. All source-observed at pinned commits.
- Counter-evidence and gap: [Mem0](../systems/source-studies/mem0.md)’s `expiration_date` is only a visibility filter, [Aelios](../systems/source-studies/aelios.md)’s trigger rebuild skips existing triggers, and [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md)’s current-state rows are not invalidated on fact supersede, so these studies do not establish expiry/withdrawal closure across all derived and wake paths. Existing visibility filters and active-state checks are partial safeguards, not evidence that no filtering occurs. No community Incident Seed and no accepted microcase.

## D-007 — Retraction without residual influence

Withdrawing or deleting a memory should remove its influence from dependent state (summaries, indexes, triggers, retained context); it should not silently keep acting on the user’s behalf.

- Failure on one side: a “deleted” fact keeps shaping summaries, retrieval or scheduled actions.
- Failure on the other: physical removal also destroys the provenance needed to audit or repair earlier decisions.
- Key test: withdraw a supported fact, then probe each derived layer and later behaviour under a full-history control.
- Source corroboration: multi-source. [Mem0](../systems/source-studies/mem0.md)’s delete is best-effort, leaves the recent-message buffer and may skip entity cleanup; [Graphiti](../systems/source-studies/graphiti.md)’s `remove_episode` keeps shared source arrays and does not restore invalidated edges; [Letta](../systems/source-studies/letta.md) cascades no deletion to transcript, summaries or restatements; [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md) has no unified forget request and its redaction step is not deletion; [Tideline Memory](../systems/source-studies/tideline-memory.md) exposes no delete tool; [Aelios](../systems/source-studies/aelios.md), [Hindsight](../systems/source-studies/hindsight.md) and [OpenViking](../systems/source-studies/openviking.md) clear different layers per entry point; [LangMem](../systems/source-studies/langmem.md) deletes only the current key. All source-observed at pinned commits.
- Remaining gap: residual influence is a static source possibility, not an observed runtime failure, and per-entry retraction closure differs across paths. No accepted microcase.

## D-008 — Acknowledgement without durable completion

A write, ingest or deletion acknowledgement should not be taken as proof that every derived state is durable, complete and consistent.

- Failure on one side: trusting a success receipt and silently losing continuity when a downstream layer failed.
- Failure on the other: discarding still-usable partial state because one acknowledgement looked incomplete.
- Key test: inject one bounded failure and compare the returned receipt with the independently observed per-layer state.
- Source corroboration: multi-source. [Mem0](../systems/source-studies/mem0.md)’s returned ADD events are not per-fact searchability and its history is not a cross-store commit proof; [OpenViking](../systems/source-studies/openviking.md)’s completed snapshot receipt does not prove every index rebuilt; [Hindsight](../systems/source-studies/hindsight.md)’s async `operation_id` means submitted, not done; [Aelios](../systems/source-studies/aelios.md) returns before the assistant write is durably confirmed; [LangMem](../systems/source-studies/langmem.md)’s remote future is a submission and its local queue is not durable; [lmc-5](../systems/source-studies/lmc-5.md) checks that evidence exists, not that it is valid; [A-MEM](../systems/source-studies/a-mem.md) keeps partial evolution writes after a captured error. All source-observed at pinned commits.
- Remaining gap: this is an observability boundary, not a measured failure rate, and each layer would need its own readiness signal. No accepted microcase.

## Promotion rule

A distinction becomes “supported” only when it has:

- at least two independent community Incident Seeds, or one Incident Seed plus strong source/system evidence;
- a bounded counterfactual;
- a plausible architecture-neutral observation boundary;
- at least one accepted microcase.

The source corroboration recorded above is necessary evidence but does not by itself satisfy any clause: two source studies are not a substitute for the community Incident Seed the first clause requires, and no distinction yet has an accepted microcase.
