## Status

- `draft` — incomplete or unreviewed;
- `reviewed` — source-fidelity review occurred, but acceptance conditions may remain open;
- `accepted` — usable by the declared Relata consumer;
- `rejected` — not adopted, with reasons preserved;
- `superseded` — retained for provenance but replaced by a linked card.

An accepted card must pin the exact object, declare read and execution coverage, separate source claims from observation and inference, state unavailable boundaries, preserve material counter-evidence, and link to a decision, assumption, distinction, case, or System Census dimension.

Cards may reference private working evidence, but private paths, restricted artifacts, credentials, and unpublished source material do not belong in a public card. Record the unavailable boundary instead.
