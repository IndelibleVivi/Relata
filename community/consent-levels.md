# Consent Modes for Community Contributions

These labels describe what may happen to one contribution. They are **not** a ladder of commitment, status, trust, or research value. A contributor may choose different modes for different artifacts and may decline any route.

Project contribution consent does not by itself satisfy any institutional process that may apply to a future formal human-participant study.

## Mode 0 — Conversation only

The material may inform the immediate private conversation. Relata does not retain it in project files, summaries, aggregate findings, or case design. If an insight later seems useful, ask for new permission before recording or using it.

## Mode 1 — Aggregate insight only

Relata may retain a generalized insight that cannot reasonably be linked in the research artifact to the contributor or a specific incident. No Incident Seed, direct quotation, public attribution, or synthetic derivation is authorized.

## Mode 2 — Restricted abstract contribution

Relata may retain a de-identified structural Incident Seed, System Card, or review in a restricted workspace. This mode does not authorize public release or synthetic case derivation.

## Mode 3 — Public synthetic derivation

A restricted abstract contribution may inform a fully synthetic adult case. The public case must not reproduce or claim to represent the source relationship. The contributor reviews the proposed derivation before publication unless a different review arrangement was explicitly recorded.

## Mode 4 — Public contribution

One specified artifact may be published with the contributor’s chosen named, pseudonymous, collective, or anonymous attribution. This mode applies only to the named artifact and does not automatically authorize raw-chat publication, direct quotation, architecture disclosure, future derivatives, or reuse in another project.

## Permissions recorded separately

For every contribution, record:

- contribution ID and artifact type;
- consent mode and permitted uses;
- attribution form;
- whether any direct quotation is permitted outside public synthetic cases;
- which architecture details may be public;
- whether synthetic derivation is permitted;
- whether pre-publication review is required and how approval is expressed;
- whether follow-up contact is permitted;
- restricted storage location, steward, and retention or review date;
- withdrawal contact and disclosed limits after public release.

Silence is not approval unless that exact mechanism was knowingly agreed in advance.

## Withdrawal

Before public release, a contributor may withdraw the contribution from the maintained project without giving a reason. Relata also removes unpublished derivatives that materially depend on it unless the contributor agrees otherwise.

After public release, maintainers can remove or deprecate material in future maintained versions, stop active use, remove identity links where feasible, and publish a correction. They cannot guarantee deletion from third-party clones, forks, archives, citations, or already distributed releases. This limit must be disclosed before publication.

## Record separation

Completed identifying consent records do not belong in this repository. Store them in a restricted location separately from de-identified research artifacts. Public provenance may state the derivation method and permission mode without exposing identity or private source material.

Use [`contribution-consent-record-template.md`](contribution-consent-record-template.md) as the field checklist, then store the completed record outside the public repository.
